## Favorite plugins
- Aerial - Takes your file or markdown and presents a table of contents on your right you can use to jump between the different headings or functions
- Render_Markdown - You can read  markdown files in nvim, it's great. It might just be  a better reason to use md than obsidian (for me).
- Neo-Tree - similar to Oil but it's not full screen the best way to imagine it is the side panel file viewer in vscode. I think it's really great for writing code.
- Vapor.nvim - I hate to be biased but can't imagine using nvim without it. My favorite file in nvim is my mardkownfile/cheat sheet it just instantly opens with one
                one hot key. And then with another hot key i can open the folder i'm working on.
